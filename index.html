Layer 01 — Roof

Your Home's
First Defense

Your roof stands between your family and everything the New England climate can throw at it — nor'easters, ice dams, and decades of freeze-thaw cycles. We install premium architectural shingles, metal roofing, and flat-roof systems using manufacturer-certified techniques that keep your warranty fully intact.

Every installation starts with a full deck inspection. Damaged sheathing, improper ventilation, and compromised flashing are addressed before the first shingle goes down — not patched over.

  • 30-year architectural shingles, IKO & GAF certified
  • Ice & water shield on all eave overhangs
  • Full deck inspection — replacement included if needed

Layer 02 — Gutters

Protect What's
Below the Roof

Most foundation and siding damage in Greater Boston traces back to water that had nowhere to go. Undersized gutters, improper slope, or failed seams send rainwater straight into your fascia boards and down your foundation walls. We size and install seamless aluminum gutter systems fabricated on-site to your home's exact measurements.

Downspout positioning is calculated for your specific lot — not placed where it's easiest. The result is clean drainage that protects your landscaping and foundation for years.

  • Seamless aluminum — no joints to fail
  • Hidden hanger system every 24 inches
  • LeafGuard micro-mesh protection available

Layer 03 — Skylights

Bring the Light
In — Leak-Free

A properly installed skylight transforms a kitchen, master bath, or living room without adding square footage. The challenge is the intersection between glass, frame, and roof — that's where most skylights fail. We are VELUX-certified installers, using manufacturer-matched flashing kits designed for your exact roof pitch and shingle profile.

Fixed, venting, and solar-powered models are all available. We handle the full project from roof opening to interior drywall finish — no separate sub-trades, no coordination headaches.

  • VELUX certified — 10-year no-leak guarantee
  • Fixed, venting & solar-powered models
  • Interior finish included — fully turnkey

Layer 04 — Siding

Curb Appeal That
Works as Hard as You

Your siding is the most visible part of your home — and its most important moisture barrier. Failing or dated siding is more than an eyesore; it lets water into your wall cavities, increasing heating bills and inviting rot. We install vinyl, James Hardie fiber cement, and engineered wood siding systems with properly lapped housewrap and sealed penetrations throughout.

Whether you need a full re-side or a targeted repair on storm-damaged sections, we match existing profiles and colors precisely. Our crews leave every site clean — no scrap in the shrubs.

  • James Hardie & premium vinyl installed
  • Insulated options available — R-3 to R-5
  • All profiles: lap, shake, board & batten
