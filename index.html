Gutters

Most gutter failures start at the seam, not the middle. We reseal or replace from the fascia out. Seamless aluminum gutters designed for New England ice loads and heavy spring rain - per EPA residential water management guidelines, properly maintained gutters are the first defense against foundation damage.

  • Seamless aluminum gutter installation

Skylights

A skylight that leaks is a flashing problem, not a glass problem. Palma installs and reseals skylights using step flashing that integrates with the roofing system, not just overlaid on top of it. New installation or reseal of existing units.

  • Integrated with full roof replacement when timing aligns
  • Fixed, venting, and tubular styles

Questions

How much does a new roof cost in Massachusetts?

In Massachusetts, most residential roof replacements run between $8,000 and $18,000 depending on square footage, pitch, and material. Asphalt shingles cost less than cedar shake. Palma Contracting provides free on-site estimates - call (781) 258-9990 to schedule.

How long does a cedar shake roof last in New England?

Cedar shake roofs last 30-40 years with proper installation and maintenance, per the Cedar Shake and Shingle Bureau. New England's climate - with freeze-thaw cycles and over 60 inches of annual snowfall per NOAA - makes proper installation essential. Palma has installed cedar shake roofs across Greater Boston since 2009.

What's the difference between vinyl, wood, and fiber cement siding?

Vinyl siding lasts 20-40 years at $3-8 per sq ft installed. Wood lasts 20-30 years but requires regular painting or staining at $6-12 per sq ft. Fiber cement such as James Hardie lasts 50+ years at $6-13 per sq ft with low maintenance and superior fire resistance, per NAHB Cost vs Value data.

Material Avg Lifespan Maintenance Cost Range Best For
Vinyl 20-40 years Low $3-8/sq ft Budget, low-maintenance
Wood 20-30 years High $6-12/sq ft Historic homes, natural look
Fiber Cement 50+ years Low-Medium $6-13/sq ft Durability, paint-hold, fire resistance

Source: NAHB Cost vs Value Report / James Hardie industry data

Do I need a permit for roof replacement in Massachusetts?

Yes. Massachusetts requires a building permit for residential roof replacement. Per the Massachusetts Division of Professional Licensure, any residential construction project over $1,000 must be performed by or under a licensed Construction Supervisor. How long does a typical roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements take 1-3 days depending on size, pitch, and material. Cedar shake takes longer than asphalt shingle due to hand-cutting and fitting. A full roof and gutter replacement has been completed in under two days in winter conditions. Palma's crews work to minimize disruption to your household.

What's the best time of year for roof replacement in Massachusetts?

Late spring through early fall (May through October) is ideal in Massachusetts - stable temperatures and lower precipitation. Roofing can be completed year-round, though. Materials and adhesives are rated for cold-weather application, and Palma's crews work in winter when schedules require it. Spring and summer slots fill fast.

What roofing materials hold up best against New England snow?

Architectural asphalt shingles with ice-and-water shield underlayment at eaves are standard for New England, where Massachusetts averages over 60 inches of annual snowfall per NOAA. Cedar shake, properly installed with breathable underlayment, achieves 30-40 year lifespans per the Cedar Shake and Shingle Bureau and handles freeze-thaw cycles well.

How often should gutters be replaced?

Residential gutters typically last 20-30 years with regular cleaning. Gutters that sag, pull from fascia, or show rust need replacement sooner. Proper drainage reduces foundation damage per EPA residential water management guidelines. Palma installs seamless aluminum gutters rated for New England ice loads.
