# Palma Contracting - Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Established homeowners in the Greater Boston Area and NH suburbs - typically 35-65, own their home, have dealt with contractors before, and are doing research online after getting a referral or finding via Google. They arrive with a specific problem (aging roof, storm damage, siding replacement) and need to quickly confirm Palma is credible, local, and worth calling. They are NOT first-time homeowners looking for the cheapest option - they want quality done right.

**Job to be done:** Assess credibility fast, then convert to a phone call or quote request.

### Brand Personality
"Solid craftsmanship, reliable service." - this IS the brand voice. No marketing fluff.

Three-word personality: **Dependable. Skilled. Local.**

Emotional goal: The homeowner should feel *confident* - not wowed or dazzled, but genuinely reassured that this company knows what they're doing and won't make their life difficult.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Style:** Editorial/premium residential - polished but not luxury-posturing. Closer to a well-designed home services brand than a corporate contractor directory.
- **Reference:** `index.html` (current landing page - Stitch-inspired asymmetric layout with navy/orange palette) and `brand-guide.html` (color system, typography standards).
- **Anti-references:** Generic contractor sites (diagonal banner stripes, stock photos of hard hats, big red "CALL NOW" buttons, low-contrast gray text). Should not look like a template anyone could buy for $29.
- **Theme:** Light mode only. The `#F8FAFC` surface with `#0F172A` navy and `#F97316`/`#C2410C` orange accent is the established palette - do not deviate.
- **Typography:** Manrope (headlines, extrabold/800 weight) + Inter (body). Tight tracking on headlines (`tracking-tight`). These are locked in.

### Design Principles

1. **Authority through restraint.** The navy/orange palette reads as confident. Don't undermine it with generic contractor clichés. No diagonal accent stripes, no glowing buttons, no hard-hat stock photos.

2. **Local specificity over generic claims.** "Serving Newton, MA since 2009" beats "serving your area." Every town SEO page should feel genuinely written for that location - mention the suburb by name in headlines and body copy, not just in the metadata.

3. **Trust signals, not marketing claims.** "Licensed & Insured, references available upon request" is more credible than "Best in the Business." Lead with verifiable facts: years in business (2009), real address (Waltham, MA), real phone number.

4. **Editorial layout, not a directory listing.** Asymmetric bento grids, varied section weights, and intentional white space distinguish this from the thousands of identical contractor sites. Use the Stitch-inspired layout patterns established in `index.html`.

5. **Mobile-first for the quote moment.** Established homeowners often research on their phone while standing in the driveway looking at damage. CTA buttons must be thumb-reachable, phone number must be a tap-to-call link (`tel:`), and forms must be simple.

## Component Standards (from brand-guide.html)

### Colors
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-primary` | `#0F172A` | Headlines, dark surfaces, primary text |
| `--color-secondary` | `#334155` | Supporting text, secondary UI |
| `--color-tertiary` | `#F97316` | Accent text, borders, star ratings |
| `tertiary-container` | `#C2410C` | CTA button backgrounds |
| `on-tertiary-container` | `#FFF7ED` | Text on CTA buttons |
| `--color-neutral` | `#F8FAFC` | Page background |

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Manrope, weight 700-800, `tracking-tight`, `leading-[1.07-1.1]`
- **Body:** Inter, weight 400-500, `leading-relaxed`
- **Labels/Eyebrows:** Inter, weight 600-700, `text-[10-11px]`, `tracking-widest`, `uppercase`

### Spacing
8px base grid. Section padding: `py-24` (96px). Container: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-6 md:px-8`.

### Border Radius
Buttons: `rounded-xl`. Cards: `rounded-2xl`. Large panels: `rounded-3xl`. Tags/pills: `rounded-full`.

## Page Architecture

### Planned Pages
- `index.html` - Main landing page (complete)
- `brand-guide.html` - Internal design reference (complete)
- Town SEO pages (planned) - One per service area town

### Town SEO Page Pattern
Each town page should follow the same structure as `index.html` but:
- Localize the `<title>` and `<meta description>` with town name
- Reference the town in the H1 headline
- Mention the town name naturally in body copy 2-3 times
- Use the same nav, trust metrics, services, and contact sections
- Footer and contact info remain identical
