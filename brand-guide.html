01 - Color System

Brand Palette

Four foundational colors with full tonal scales. Primary and Secondary anchor the UI; Tertiary provides energy and emphasis.

#0F172A

Primary

Deep navy. Authority, trust, and precision. Used for headlines, primary surfaces, and high-emphasis UI elements.

#334155

Secondary

Slate. Supports Primary as a softer dark tone for secondary text, borders, and background variations.

#F97316

Tertiary

Burnt orange. Energy, action, and warmth. Used for CTAs, highlights, and brand accents only - never as a background.

#F8FAFC

Neutral

Cool off-white. Page backgrounds, surfaces, and dividers. Tinted toward Primary to maintain palette cohesion.

06 - Usage Guidelines

Color Usage

Rules for maintaining visual hierarchy and brand consistency across all touchpoints.

Do

Use Tertiary (#F97316) for primary CTAs and key highlights only.

Use Primary (#0F172A) as the dominant surface and text color on light backgrounds.

Pair Neutral (#F8FAFC) as the page background to keep contrast high.

Use Secondary (#334155) for supporting text, labels, and secondary actions.

Don't

Don't use Tertiary as a background color across large areas.

Don't place gray text on colored backgrounds - use a tint of the background color.

Don't use pure black (#000) or pure white (#fff) - always use a palette value.

Don't combine all four brand colors in a single component - pick two maximum.
