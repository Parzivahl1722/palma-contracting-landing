Roofing

Palma Contracting installs and replaces residential and commercial roofing systems across the Greater Boston Area, including cedar shake, barn, and standard asphalt-shingle roofs. All work is performed by a licensed Massachusetts Construction Supervisor and built to withstand New England weather - from freeze-thaw cycles to 60+ inches of annual snowfall per NOAA climate data.

  • check_circle Barn & Agricultural Roofs
  • check_circle Residential Roof Replacement
Residential & Commercial

Siding

Palma Contracting installs vinyl, wood, and fiber cement siding on residential homes throughout the Greater Boston Area. Fiber cement siding - such as James Hardie - achieves 50+ year lifespans with low maintenance per NAHB Cost vs Value data. We help you select the right material for your home's architecture and New England climate demands.

  • check_circle Full Siding Replacement
  • check_circle Partial Repair & Matching
  • check_circle Vinyl, Wood & Fiber Cement
Residential

Gutters

Palma Contracting installs seamless aluminum gutter systems designed for New England's rainfall and ice loads. Properly maintained gutters reduce water-related foundation damage per EPA residential water management guidelines. We also handle repairs, resealing, and downspout extensions for existing systems.

  • check_circle Seamless Gutter Installation
  • check_circle Gutter Repair & Resealing
  • check_circle Downspout Extensions
